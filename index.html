Core Premise

A.E.G.I.S. is a superhero science-fantasy tabletop setting about a universe where state-controlled order has replaced independent heroism. The multiverse collapsed into a single Prime Universe. In the aftermath, six galaxies unified for survival — but the emergency authority that saved civilization refused to give power back.

The central conflict is not simply heroes versus villains. It is heroism versus militarized control. A.E.G.I.S. teaches that independent heroes are terrorists. The Vigilantes believe A.E.G.I.S. has turned protection into obedience and hope into propaganda.

Running Tone

The setting should feel like superhero myth after the state won. A.E.G.I.S. is not cartoon evil — it saved civilization, then used that victory to justify permanent control. The Vigilantes are not clean heroes — they bleed, kill, argue, and break laws, but they protect people directly. Old heroes are not nostalgia only: their choices, sins, and betrayals are still alive.

The core question: Who gets to decide whether power is a curse, a weapon, a crime, or a gift?

Species & Power Blocs

Wargs & the Wolf Dominion

Wargs are genetically diverse anthropomorphic beings, each lineage tied to an animal archetype. They are physically superior to baseline humans, naturally inclined toward lineage traits, and socially engineered into rigid roles. The Lupine High Houses govern Warg society across the galaxies, enforce biological destiny, and work directly with A.E.G.I.S. and P.A.R.A.G.O.N. Mutation inside noble wolf houses is viewed as weakness and danger, not evolution.

The IX Royal House

The IX are male Warg princes from a powerful non-wolf royal house — the only non-wolf house holding real power beside the Lupine High Houses. Their numbering ranks power from IX-100 to IX-1, with IX-1 the strongest. Known princes: IX-66 and IX-77 (IX-66 ranks above IX-77). Core traits: extreme strength, durability, regeneration, royal arrogance, and predatory warrior culture.

GM Spoiler
Key weakness revealed: HellKnight's black hellfire prevents IX regeneration and terrifies them.

Alder

Ancient, long-lived humanoids: centuries-to-millennia lifespans, refined intellect, near-perfect physical control, and deep connection to cosmic and arcane forces.

GM Spoiler
The Alder are old enough and perceptive enough to suspect Sovereign may be approximately 2,000 years old. Elegantari is an Alder and the father of Mythos — his forbidden union with Athena is a major secret.

Brightons

Sentient energy-based beings born from a star system that was birthplace, power source, and collective anchor. Semi-corporeal, radiant, luminous, emotionally intense, constantly shifting. Their home galaxy is dying; most survivors have left to join or shelter with the Alder. SunBurst was a Brighton bound to a battle mech — a living star-being turned fast, witty, radiant war machine.

Mycelyth

Fungal-based collective beings with semi-humanoid bodies and vast underground/subspace networks. They share consciousness across distances but are not mindless — a living ecosystem with thought.

Graven

Stone- and metal-bodied beings infused with ancient energy. Each carries memory cores of ancestors, wars, knowledge, and ancient truths. Killing a Graven can destroy a person, a bloodline, and a library of history.

Kurathi / Kaerathi

The same race under different spellings and cultural usage — Kurathi is used for Athena, Enforcer, and the imperial/political context. Genetically perfected warrior beings: superior physical genetics, regeneration, flight, kinetic manipulation, gravimetric weaponry, adaptive warframes, and conquest-driven culture. Their civilization refused full A.E.G.I.S. integration, but Kurathi power centers and Earth government representatives remain important diplomatic players.

AEGIS D10 Core Rulebook — Compilation Edition

AEGIS uses ten-sided dice in pools. Ordinary actions roll Attribute + Skill dice. Direct Power actions roll the governing Attribute + Power Rating. When a Power enhances a skilled action, roll Attribute + Skill and apply only the Technique's listed enhancement — do not add the full Power Rating.

This page is a quick reference. The complete rulebook (21 chapters: powers, equipment, mass combat, lairs, and more) is on the Downloads page.

Successes, 1s, 10s & Botches

• Natural 10s explode recursively — roll another die for each natural 10; new 10s also explode.
• A natural 1 cancels one success.
• A botch occurs when more than half of the original rolled dice show natural 1s. Explosion dice never count toward botch determination, and a 1 on an explosion die cancels a success but cannot create a botch.
• Zero or negative pool: roll one desperation die — it botches on 1 and explodes on 10.
• Traits above 10: roll a maximum of 10 dice per trait; each full point above 10 becomes one automatic success.

Opposed rolls: net successes = attacker's final successes minus defender's. One or more net successes wins; defender wins ties. 5+ net successes is a Critical Success. Ordinary modifiers cap at +5/−5; Teamwork dice cap at +5 separately (helpers with 2+ final successes grant the leader +1 die each).

Willpower

Maximum Willpower = Resolve + Composure. One Willpower per roll; normally one on your own turn plus one on a Reaction before your next turn. Spend it to: add +3 dice before a roll · reroll up to three failed dice after a roll · resist compulsion, emotion, a defining Flaw, or a prohibited command · remain conscious or active one turn · reroll a failed Strain-control test · ignore wound penalties until next turn. A Short Rest restores 1 Willpower; a Full Rest restores all.

Combat Basics

A round is ~6 seconds. Per round: one Standard, one Movement, one Minor, one Reaction, and reasonable Free Actions. Repeated attacks on your own turn take −3 / −6 / −9 exceptional penalties for the second, third, and fourth attacks.

Canon Corrections

• "JSA" is not a valid term in this setting. The original JSS was the independent Justice Society of Sovereigns; the modern JSS is the A.E.G.I.S.-sponsored successor that leads P.A.R.A.G.O.N.

• A.E.G.I.S. is not ARGOS. ARGOS is an old draft term, intelligence error, or discarded file name unless intentionally reintroduced as an outdated codename.

• Harbinger does not have true phasing. Any apparent phasing is shadow-based displacement known as Shadow-Slip.

• Kurathi remain excluded from standard player species; Mythos is the sole exception.

GM Spoiler — Sovereign, Key Truths

He is ~2,000 years old; only the Alder suspect it. He is the last of his kind. His people built a weapon capable of annihilating galaxies — fearing they would use it, he destroyed his own species. He swore never to kill again and has kept that oath. His power and speed are unmatched; he can kill Kurathi without difficulty. He could destroy A.E.G.I.S. by himself, but refuses to become the destroyer again.

GM Spoiler — Athena, Elegantari & Mythos

Athena is Kurathi. Elegantari is Alder. Mythos is their son — a forbidden Kurathi-Alder hybrid whose existence would be a death sentence if either side learned who and what he is. Athena trained Mythos with extreme discipline because loss of control could expose his heritage and catastrophic power. Her doctrine, "Emotion destroys worlds," is both emotional damage and survival strategy.

Current Strategic Plan: The Quiet Rebellion

GM / Campaign-Group Only

Sovereign's plan is not immediate open war. The Vigilantes will turn heroes against A.E.G.I.S. slowly, carefully, and with evidence:

1. Find heroes and P.A.R.A.G.O.N. loyalists with doubts.
2. Show them evidence slowly rather than forcing immediate public war.
3. Reveal A.E.G.I.S. lies one by one — especially around Boom, the JSS, Blackgate, and hidden experiments.
4. Protect defectors before A.E.G.I.S. can erase them.
5. Build enough internal fracture that A.E.G.I.S. begins to fall from disbelief, not only force.

Player Introduction

The year is 3025. Fifty years ago, the multiverse collapsed toward a single universe. Galaxies united to survive. The authority created to save them never gave its power back.

A.E.G.I.S. calls independent heroes a threat. P.A.R.A.G.O.N. supplies the sanctioned answer. The old Justice Society of Sovereigns was destroyed in a war the whole civilization watched, and most people know only the version the victors broadcast.

You live among people who remember another story. Some carry powers inherited from bloodlines or distant stars. Others built their strength, learned forbidden arts, or survived something designed to make them a weapon. A few still try to help people without asking permission.

Every operation asks more than whether you can beat the enemy. Who gets out alive? Who holds the evidence? What do the people you rescue believe afterward? How much of yourself will you risk to keep someone else free?

The original JSS (the independent Justice Society of Sovereigns), the modern JSS (the A.E.G.I.S.-sponsored institution), and the JSS capability tier are three distinct things. A character can possess JSS-level power while fighting against the institution that uses the same name.

The Campaign's Central Pressures

Protection and control. A.E.G.I.S. can point to real disasters and dangerous superhumans. The Vigilantes must show that another way of protecting people can work.

Legacy and ownership. The state claims the JSS legacy. Boom's example, the original Clockwork Angel's sacrifice, Atomica's preservation of Mercy, and Mastermind's survival give the resistance a different inheritance.

Bloodline and identity. Warg lineage, Alder persecution, and Kurathi imperial honor shape lives and threaten outsiders.

Power and consequence. Extraordinary powers have boundaries. Victory can leave prisoners, damaged ley lines, displaced civilians, lost equipment, or an injured witness who still needs protection.

The core question: Who gets to decide whether power is a curse, a weapon, a crime, or a gift?

History of the Prime Universe

The Prime Convergence

Roughly fifty years before the current campaign, the multiverse collapsed toward a single Prime Universe. Reality tore open. Populations crossed boundaries that had previously separated entire universes. Variants, displaced peoples, unfamiliar powers, and physical anomalies became part of ordinary survival — and gave governments a reason to fear poorly understood power.

The Unity War and Permanent Emergency

The galaxies united to survive. A.E.G.I.S. emerged as central command during the Unity War. When survival gave way to stability, emergency authority remained. Superhuman force became a military resource; the Zero Ascendancy Policy prohibits independent superhuman power from rising outside sanctioned authority.

The Fall of the Original JSS

About twenty-five years ago, the Justice Society of Sovereigns refused A.E.G.I.S. authority. The Sovereign Uprising became the Broadcast War — a public confrontation transmitted across the six galaxies, with the heroes framed as enemies of peace. The broadcast concealed what the heroes were protecting. Boom defended evacuation corridors while suppression weakened him. Young Vendetta witnessed a death that would shape his life. The team did not meet a single uniform fate: some died, some survived captivity, some were erased from public memory.

The Propaganda Era

The modern A.E.G.I.S.-sponsored JSS occupies the public place once held by independent heroes and leads P.A.R.A.G.O.N. An honest sanctioned hero can still serve an institution that lies about its founding violence. The rebellion therefore needs more than victories: it needs witnesses who can survive disclosure, evidence that cannot be easily dismissed, and places where defectors can stay alive.

Peoples

Humans

Humans participate at every level, from civilian life to overwhelming superhuman power. A human origin does not imply a low ceiling — Mastermind remains human despite being the strongest psychic ever recorded. Mutation, cosmic inheritance, technology, occult instruction, and experimentation create different counters even when two Powers look similar.

Wargs

A diverse animal-derived people: wolves, leonine chimeras, reptilians, birds, aquatic forms, and more. The Wyld is the civilization; the Wolf Dominion is its present political regime. Biological Lineage and political House are distinct — senses, wings, or natural weapons do not confer nobility, and adoption, conquest, marriage, debt, exile, and alliance can change affiliation without changing biology.

Alder

Alder, Aldar, Eldar, and Elves are the same species. The first spacefaring and conquering species in their history, grown from three ancestral bloodlines (Trolyska, Gar'aske, Umbrsyieg). True-Blood Alder approach seven feet and are functionally immortal, not considered adults until about 100. Engineered Alder average six feet and live roughly a century due to a design flaw. The Wasting Disease means only about one in fifty naturally born children survives the first year. Thirty Houses, five Great Houses, and the Arcane Eight govern — and only True-Bloods debate and vote. The Alder backed A.E.G.I.S. expansion and hold its highest positions. Arska's prophecy of the Dark Prophet, held infallible, justifies absolute prohibition of hybrids.

Brightons

Sentient energy beings whose star system was birthplace, power source, and collective anchor. Their luminous forms shift with emotion. A Brighton's Heartflare is a continuity anchor — destruction of an outer form need not end the person while it survives. Their dying homeland has driven migration to Alder shelter.

Mycelyth

Fungal beings whose networks extend through underground and subspace environments. Shared consciousness does not make them mindless. A colony, spore node, or biomass anchor can carry continuity across bodily destruction — so isolation is more than loneliness, and contamination can threaten more than one body.

Graven

Living stone-and-metal beings sustained by ancient energy and memory cores carrying inherited knowledge, wars, names, and histories. They rely on repair rather than biological healing. Resistant to poison, hunger, disease, and suffocation — but not to corrosion, electricity, pressure, heat, force, or attacks on the memory core.

Kurathi / Kaerathi

Alternate spellings of one species: imperial warrior culture, exceptional physical genetics, regeneration, longevity, flight, kinetic capabilities, gravimetric technology, and noble Houses. Not a standard player species — Mythos is the established playable exception.

Primaris

Sovereign is the last surviving Primaris. Their biology, culture, and wider history remain reserved; there is no playable Primaris template.

Institutions

General Silver (also Doctor Silver) leads P.A.R.A.G.O.N. and sits on the A.E.G.I.S. Council, holding multiple doctorates and substantial military, scientific, and political authority.

Blackgate Omega was the most secure superhuman prison in the eastern galaxies; its collapse released 93 inmates and led into the Saint Lazarus crisis. Other black programs imprison, experiment upon, and classify people as assets or property.

Occult factions: the Salem Coven is a hunted group of mystic heroes and potential ally. The Second Dynasty was Gui Huang's undead imperial project at Fengdu.

When portraying A.E.G.I.S., distinguish institutions from individuals. A medic, investigator, or loyal young hero may believe sincerely in public protection — this gives the Quiet Rebellion people to persuade, not just installations to destroy.

AEGIS D10 Play Reference

AEGIS uses ten-sided dice in pools. Ordinary actions roll Attribute + Skill. Direct Power actions roll governing Attribute + Power Rating. When a Power enhances skilled work, roll Attribute + Skill and add only the Technique's listed benefit — never all three ratings.

The full 21-chapter Core Rulebook (equipment, conditions, digital conflict, projects, safe havens, mass combat) is on the Downloads page.

Building a Roll

• Each die showing 8, 9, or 10 is one success.
• Natural 10s explode recursively.
• Each natural 1 cancels a success, including a 1 rolled on an explosion die.
• Botch: more than half the original dice show 1s. Explosion dice cannot create a botch.
• Zero or negative pool: roll one desperation die (botches on 1, explodes on 10).

Traits Above 10

Each trait contributes at most ten rolled dice; its own points above ten become automatic successes. Dexterity 10 + Firearms 8 = 18 dice. Dexterity 13 + Firearms 8 = 18 dice + 3 automatic successes.

Modifier Order

Build the pool → convert over-ten trait points → cancel ordinary bonuses and penalties → apply the ordinary cap of ±5 → add Teamwork separately (max +5) → apply exceptional modifiers such as Scale.

Opposed Tests & Difficulty

Subtract the defender's successes from the attacker's; the defender wins ties. One net success wins; five net successes is a Critical Success, with another benefit per additional full five at GM discretion.

DifficultyBenchmark
1Routine task under pressure
2Standard trained challenge
3Challenging professional action
5Exceptional specialist achievement
7Legendary achievement
10+Civilization-scale or seemingly impossible action

Teamwork: choose a leader; each helper with 2+ final successes grants one die, max +5 total. Extended actions accumulate successes — thresholds / max intervals: Minor 5/3, Standard 10/5, Complex 15/6, Major 25/8, Legendary 40/10. A botch removes 3 progress and adds a complication.

A Combat Round

~6 seconds. Each character gets one Standard, one Movement, one Minor, one Reaction (refreshes at the start of your turn), and reasonable Free Actions. A Full action consumes Standard + Movement. Repeated attacks on your own turn take −3 / −6 / −9 exceptional penalties. Extra actions need explicit permission — at most one extra of a given type per turn.

Attacks & Damage

Raw Damage = Base Damage + net successes + any Critical damage benefit. Then apply Protection, Armor Piercing, resistance, vulnerability, immunity, and Scale.

• Protection: highest source in full + half the second-highest, rounded down. AP reduces combined Protection to a minimum of 0.
• Critical can: +3 Raw Damage, +2 AP, impose a condition, increase knockback, disrupt a resource, damage equipment, or avoid collateral.
• Resistance halves remaining damage (round down); Vulnerability +50% (round up); Severe Vulnerability doubles; Immunity zeroes. Ordinary Protection is halved against Annihilation.
• An area attack is rolled once and compared to each target's defense.

Damage TypeRecovery & Consequence
SuperficialCannot reduce Health below 0; continued damage converts existing Superficial to Lethal.
LethalNatural recovery 1 per 24 hours. Bleeding may continue until treated.
AggravatedNatural recovery 1 per week. Enhanced healing needs explicit permission.
AnnihilationNo ordinary healing. Reconstruction needs explicit permission and surviving continuity.

Wounded (½ Health or less): −1 to physical Skill and direct Power actions. Critical (¼ or less): −2 to all actions, Movement down one step. 0 Health: incapacitated. Negative Health = Stamina: Dying (Stamina + Survival diff 2 each round). Negative = 2× Stamina: Dead unless a continuity rule applies. First aid: Intelligence + Medicine diff 2. Damage ≥ ½ max Health after mitigation is Massive; ≥ ¾ is Catastrophic.

Reserve, Strain & Willpower

Powers spend Energy from Power Reserve. Recover 1 Reserve at the start of your turn only if you spent none since your previous turn and paid no upkeep.

Strain: at ⅓ Strain Limit, direct Power and control rolls take −1 exceptional die. At ⅔, −2 and Power costs +1 Energy. At the limit you Crash: Reserve to 0, Power Crashed and Exhausted, non-survival sustained effects end, and Lethal damage equal to ¼ current Strain (round up, max 8).

Concentration capacity = 1 + Resolve ÷ 5 (round down, max 4); each effect after the first adds −2 exceptional dice. Taking damage prompts Resolve + Composure (diff 2; 3 for Massive, 5 for Catastrophic).

Overcharge: +1 effective rating for 2 Energy & 1 Strain; +2 for 5 Energy & 3 Strain; +3 for 9 Energy & 6 Strain. Cannot exceed 15 or invent a missing permission.

Willpower (max Resolve + Composure): +3 dice before a roll, or reroll up to 3 failed dice after; also resistance, momentary continued action, control, or ignoring wounds. One per roll; normally one on your turn and one on a Reaction.

Dreadcraft

Dreadcraft controls, manipulates, creates, and destroys emotions — fear is one application, not the whole discipline. The emotion changes; memories and knowledge stay intact. It does not implant beliefs, read thoughts, rewrite memories, or dictate behavior, and magically created affection never establishes consent, allegiance, or trust. Intensity steps: absent → faint → strong → overwhelming.

• The original JSS has ten confirmed members. White Seraph and Vendetta are not retroactive members; ThunderBringer was Sovereign's sidekick, not one of the ten.

• The original Clockwork Angel's name "Eleanor" is superseded. The deceased IX Prince is IX-66 (earlier "IX-55" is superseded).

• Harbinger's Shadow-Slip is shadow displacement, not true phasing. Vendetta's density rules do not grant unrestricted passage through matter.

• ThunderBringer's historical "9+" threat rating is earlier notation — not a ninth class in the current 0–8 Threat Class table.

The Original Justice Society of Sovereigns

Before A.E.G.I.S. controlled the stars, there were heroes who acted without permission. The original JSS has ten confirmed members. A.E.G.I.S. teaches that they became too dangerous and had to be stopped. Older people, hidden communities, and outlaw heroes tell a different story.

GM Spoiler — The Fall

Sonic dampeners and nanobites weakened Boom; Thought delivered the final blow. Boom forgave him and gave the people a final thumbs-up. At Fengdu, the freed Boom recognized Vendetta, called him "Champ," said he was proud, and helped destroy the remaining amulets before dissolving — a completed farewell, not a resurrection. Thought killed Connor; his motives remain a mystery to be found in play. Lunar Fang's House tried to kill him; Athena saved him. At Fengdu, a bound "Black Brute" manifestation appeared with three copies — its relationship to the original remains unresolved. Mercy was Atomica's lover.

GM Spoiler — Sovereign

Sovereign's true name is Sa'virn. He is roughly 2,000 years old; he destroyed his own species to stop a galaxy-annihilating weapon and swore never to kill again. He found Mastermind barely alive in Wild Space. Reuniting the old team is not a restoration button — survivors carry injuries, grief, and changed ethics.

The Current Cast

Vendetta

FRONTLINE PROTECTOR / VANGUARD

Powers: Density Manipulation, Regeneration, Super Stamina, Berserker Engine; firearms and disciplined close combat.

Held Saint Lazarus Medical Center for sixteen hours after Blackgate Omega's collapse and defeated twenty-three villains. Victory is measured by the people behind him: "No. But they didn't get you."

Harbinger

EXPLOSIVE SHADOW FIGHTER / VANGUARD

Powers: Regeneration, Living Detonation, Shadow Dominion, and an unstable Cosmic Surge. Techniques include Shotgun, Veins of the Night, Dead Zone, Shadow-Slip, and Shadowform.

Medical and biological specialist and dangerous field negotiator.

Weakness: Tuned resonance can disrupt both his recovery and biological detonations.

Kitsune

TECHNICAL PRODIGY / INVESTIGATOR

Powers: Chaos Control (Glamour, Fortune), Telekinesis (Tele Swarm), Super Intelligence (Disconnect).

Nanotechnology specialist, investigator, and covert controller.

Restricted truth: From another universe, not a native Fox Warg. A dormant cosmic entity is concealed within her; she is unaware of it and cannot command it. Emergence track: silence → echoes → stirring → contact → emergence → awakening → ascendance.

Mythos

AETHER PRACTITIONER / KINETIC FIGHTER

Powers: Aether Weaving (Mind, Seer, Gate, Arbiter), Telekinesis, Super Endurance, Force Fields, Dreadcraft 1. Techniques: Aether Step, Temporal Anchor, Spatial Lock, Dimensional Counter, Sands of Time.

Gear: Phase-Edge Blades, KX-9 Graveline rifle, adaptive warframe, Alder Synaptic Crown, kinetic storage, dimensional anchor beacon.

Secret: Kurathi-Alder hybrid, son of Athena and Elegantari. Official files retain Black Division history. Killed Ajax Prime at Nightworld after losing control and fears enjoying what he could become. Sonic vulnerability; Alder metabolic restriction against meat.

White Seraph

AERIAL PROTECTOR / SCOUT / RESCUER

Powers: Living Plumage (Whisperfeather, Merciful Volley, Guardian Pinions, Pinion Cage), Flight, Super Strength, Regeneration, healing-light support.

White-furred Regalius IX Warg with large white wings. Her detachable feathers act as remote limbs and sensors. Base: the Eyrie.

Cover: Her planned marriage return to House IX is an investigative ruse at Levea's request.

Komodo

FIELD PARTNER / PACK-BROTHER

Powers: Exceptional strength, durability, regeneration, natural armor, enhanced senses, claws, tail strike, venomous bite, thermal resistance.

Reptilian Warg; Vendetta's chosen pack-brother. His loyalty is personal and earned.

Clockwork Angel

PROTECTOR / BATTLEFIELD STABILIZER

Systems: Mechanical wings, chest core, temporal shielding, field manipulation, kinetic barriers, chrono-reactive armor, biometric stabilization, light constructs.

Bearer of her mother's legacy. Ammunition, maintenance, and damaged systems matter even in extraordinary battles.

GraveSaint

HEALER / AVENGER / PRISON-BREAKER

Powers: Quantum Restoration, Quantum Reconstitution (return after death), Martyr's Evolution, Quantum Lock (traps everyone inside; stops regeneration and most adaptation — including his own).

Gear: the sword Absolution, the bow Vigil, The Last Shield, field medical kit, fragments of old JSS records.

Formerly Mercy of the original JSS. His captors exploited his healing through repeated killing; he carved names into his skin. His war now targets black sites — and has reached A.E.G.I.S.-linked families.

ThunderBringer / StormBringer

SOVEREIGN'S FORMER SIDEKICK

Powers: Atmospheric control, lightning, wind, pressure, temperature manipulation, flight, storm amplification — reaching global weather.

Sovereign

RESTORED LEADER / LIVING WITNESS

The last Primaris. Exceptional power restrained by a no-kill oath. He leads the Quiet Rebellion toward patient recruitment and evidence.

Mastermind

ORIGINAL JSS PSYCHIC / RECOVERING

Powers: Psychic Power (normally Telekinesis, Pyrotechnics, Psychic Shaping — armor, weapons, constructs in dark teal energy) and learned Aethercraft.

Human; the strongest psychic ever recorded. Sarcastic, flippant, irreverent — even near death.

Origin: His mother, Maliere Pendragon, and a coven of 22 witches poured power into him before birth, meaning him to kill the Elder Being Dagon — with a hidden curse to dispose of him after. Maliere bargained with Dagon, who destroyed the coven, married her, and at 18 took Mastermind as apprentice and slave. At 25 a Fae prince's de-enchantment revealed the manipulation; he drained Dagon's power and later stole Asmodeus's ever-changing pocket manor.

Adversaries & Major Figures

HellKnight

INFERNAL REVENGE FORCE

His black hellfire prevents regeneration, including that of IX Princes. Saved Clockwork Angel because her mother once saved him. An ally through particular bonds, not a dependable subordinate.

Athena's former lover and Connor's father. Thought killed Connor and banished him to Hell for fifty years.

Balakor

ANCIENT ONE / MASTER OF DREADCRAFT

Known to the Alder as the Walker of Insanity. Wears a broken black-and-gold mask. His manifested presence is an Aether projection; his true presence would be catastrophic to local reality. Patient, precise, alien in scale.

Dreadnought

DECEASED

Eight-foot German combatant. Diamond Flesh hardened his body; the Dreadnought Engine made him larger and more feral. Killed by Vendetta.

Whispers

DECEASED — MODERN JSS

Teleporting modern-JSS member under P.A.R.A.G.O.N. control. Killed by Harbinger's cage of shadow and blood after unmasking as a surprisingly young man. Last words: "Look for the Silver and you will find the Mole!"

Gui Huang, the Corpse Emperor

DEFEATED

World-Class undead sorcerer who sought a global undead empire, the Second Dynasty, from Fengdu Ghost City and Ming Mountain. Commanded jiangshi, ghosts, a paper legion, corpse generals, and fallen metahumans. Killed; the Ghost Gate is closed.

Vharaxis & the Brood

ELIMINATED (PER FINAL SCAN)

Female reptilian kaiju whose corpse released dozens of eight-foot juveniles with shared regeneration and adaptation. Stopped under GraveSaint's Quantum Lock.

The Alostherians (Alos)

AETHER-NATIVE SCAVENGERS

Eyeless, eel-like beings that feed on Aether and damage ley lines. Ecological, not malicious. Seven were returned home after the Brooklyn crisis.

JaxK, called Scourge

EXILED KURATHI HEIR

Destroyed his homeworld; exiled on Earth for fifty years under a containment ring. His powers have begun returning.

Suggested Next Sessions GM

The Silver: Vendetta, Harbinger, and HellKnight compare the exact wording, circumstances, and timing of their information. Choose one traceable lead — a communication route, assignment record, handler, authorization chain, or surviving equipment. Decide what evidence exists before players choose an approach.

House IX return: Begin at the New York Warg Embassy with the agreed pickup (seven days after arrangement, in campaign time) as the scene clock. White Seraph travels under a marriage cover with two companions presented as bodyguards. Make the first obstacle about access or credibility. Keep Blue alive; keep IX-66's death and Fulbritr's grievance distinct from the massacre unless play connects them. Keep separate: who carried out the massacre, who benefits, and who can prove it.

Mastermind after the rescue: A quiet recovery scene with Vendetta and White Seraph. Let him control who stays, what is examined, and when questions begin. His first concern can be practical or irreverent.

Mythos after Brooklyn: He recognizes strong fear or anger during a minor crisis, lowers it one step, and still must choose what to do. A future Balakor lesson requires new negotiation.

Fengdu & Vharaxis: Preserve the finality of Boom's and the original Clockwork Angel's release. Possible directions: trapped spirits, necromantic objects, missing prisoners, the bound Lunar Fang. Zero brood signatures reflects the scan at that moment — neither a hidden egg nor proof of none elsewhere.

Rewards: ~3 group XP for a substantial mission, plus more for a primary objective, major risk or sacrifice, and a milestone. Typical missions 3–5; finales 6–12. Story awards (like Dreadcraft 1) are recorded directly, not charged.

Quiet Rebellion Strategy GM

1. Find heroes and P.A.R.A.G.O.N. loyalists with doubts. 2. Show them evidence slowly. 3. Reveal A.E.G.I.S. lies one by one. 4. Protect defectors before they can be erased. 5. Build enough internal fracture that A.E.G.I.S. falls from disbelief, not only force. Rescue creates trust; safe houses preserve it; reckless disclosure can get a potential ally killed.
